**Wanted.** The Phase 8b summary for any range of days — asked for from the dashboard or with a
Telegram action — not only for a calendar month.

**Why it is not scheduled.** The operator chose the calendar month as 8b's only period (SS-3 in
`docs/specs/2026-10-08-spending-summary-design.md`) and was unsure a range needs a comparison at all —
perhaps just the analysis, or a comparison with the previous month or some baseline. That choice is
the open part. The calculation itself takes a date range already; what a range is compared with, and
how it is asked for, are not designed.
